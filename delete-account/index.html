Delete your data

This is the data deletion page for K-pop Empire: Build Your Agency. Use this address when a store asks for a data deletion URL.

There is no online account

This version of the game does not create an account with us. You play as a guest. Your agency, Stars, items, and purchase record are stored on your device. We do not receive a copy, and we do not have a cloud profile to erase.

Sign in with Apple, Google, or Facebook is not in this version.

Delete the save in the game

  1. Open K-pop Empire.
  2. Tap the gear icon.
  3. Tap Delete save.
  4. Tap Delete forever.

This removes that agency from the device and starts over. It cannot be undone.

Delete the app's files

Uninstall the game. That removes the game's private files from the device.

On Android you can clear them without uninstalling:

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap Apps.
  3. Tap K-pop Empire.
  4. Tap Storage.
  5. Tap Clear storage. Some phones say Clear data.

On iPhone or iPad, deleting the app removes its files.

Purchases stay with the store

Deleting the save or the app does not cancel a subscription and does not refund a purchase. Those stay on your Apple ID or Google account.

To get purchases back, install the game again on a device signed in to that same store account, open the Shop, and tap Restore. The one time gift of 300 Stars from Pro Agency is not granted a second time if the game's files were still on the device.

To cancel a subscription, follow the steps on the Support page. Cancel on the store where you subscribed. Deleting the game does not cancel it.

Refunds are requested from Apple or Google.

Ask us by email

If you emailed us, or you believe we hold any information about you, write to support@kpopempire.net with the subject "Delete my data".

Tell us the store you use (Apple or Google) and the approximate date you played or wrote to us. Do not send passwords, card numbers, or a photo of an identity document.

We will reply and delete anything we hold. In this version we do not hold player accounts, cloud saves, or analytics profiles.

If cloud save is added later

If a future version adds sign in or cloud save, this same page stays the place to ask for deletion. We will update the Privacy Policy before that version is released, including how deletion works for the new account.

More help is on the Support page.
