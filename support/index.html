Questions

How do purchases work?

The game is free to download. You can buy optional items inside the game. Apple or Google charges you, and the price you confirm on the store sheet is the price you pay. The game shows that price in your local currency. We do not see your card or your billing address.

Stars are the premium currency. VIP Agency and Empire Elite are subscriptions. Pro Agency is a one time purchase. There are also outfits, stages, expansions, and other optional items.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open the game and go to the Shop. Tap Restore.

Stay signed in to the same Apple ID, or the same Google account, that you used when you paid. The store tells the game what that account owns. If something is missing, wait a moment and tap Restore again. If it is still missing, email us with the store and the approximate date. Do not send card numbers.

Restoring brings back purchases. It does not move your agency from one phone to another.

I was charged and the item did not show up

Tap Restore in the Shop first. If the item is still missing, email us. Include the store, the item name, and the approximate date. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google, not by us.

Is my progress saved?

Yes. The game keeps one save on that device. It saves after each in game day, and when you leave the game. You play as a guest. There is no account to sign in to.

Can I continue on another phone, or on both iPhone and Android?

Not in this version. The save stays on the device. It is not uploaded. Sign in with Apple, Google, or Facebook is not in the game yet, so progress does not follow you to a new phone, and it does not move between iPhone and Android.

A purchase stays with the Apple ID or Google account that paid. Install the game on another device that uses that same store account, then tap Restore in the Shop. That restores the purchase on that store. It does not copy your agency across.

If a later version adds cloud save, we will update the Privacy Policy before that version is released.

What is Pro Agency?

Pro Agency is a one time purchase. The US list price is $4.99. The store shows the price in your currency before you pay.

Owning Pro Agency gives you:

  • An exclusive HQ decoration
  • A Pro supporter badge
  • An exclusive Pro stage outfit
  • A one time welcome gift of 300 Stars
  • A gold Pro frame on your HQ name

The 300 Stars are granted once on that device. Restoring purchases, or installing the game again while its files are still on the device, does not grant the Stars a second time.

What do VIP Agency and Empire Elite include?

These are optional subscriptions. US list prices:

  • VIP Agency: $7.99 a month, or $59.99 a year
  • Empire Elite: $19.99 a month, or $149.99 a year

The game shows the store's local price before you confirm.

VIP Agency includes 2× game speed, 30 Stars a day, 4 free pulls a day, and 2 Gold Tickets a month.

Empire Elite includes 4× game speed, Skip to tomorrow, 80 Stars a day, 5 free pulls a day, 4 Gold Tickets a month, and the expansion content while the subscription is active.

Faster speed changes how quickly a day passes. It does not change the results.

On Google Play, a new subscriber can start VIP monthly or VIP yearly with a 7 day free trial. Empire Elite does not include that trial. If a trial is offered, the store shows it before you confirm. Any unused part of a free trial ends when you buy a subscription, where the store says so.

A subscription keeps renewing until you cancel it. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends if you do not want the next charge. The store can charge the renewal during the 24 hours before the period ends.

When a subscription ends, the daily Stars, the extra free pulls, the extra Gold Tickets, the faster speed, and Elite extras stop. Expansion content that you only had through Elite stays in your save and waits until you subscribe again or buy that expansion. Pro Agency, if you own it, keeps its own perks. Items you already own stay.

How do I cancel on iPhone or iPad?
  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap your name.
  3. Tap Subscriptions.
  4. Tap the K-pop Empire subscription.
  5. Tap Cancel Subscription.

You can also open the App Store, tap your picture, then tap Subscriptions.

Cancel in Apple's settings if you subscribed on an iPhone or iPad. Deleting the game does not cancel the subscription.

How do I cancel on Android?
  1. Open the Google Play Store.
  2. Tap your profile picture.
  3. Tap Payments and subscriptions.
  4. Tap Subscriptions.
  5. Tap the K-pop Empire subscription.
  6. Tap Cancel subscription.

Cancel in Google Play if you subscribed on Android. Deleting the game does not cancel the subscription. The wording on the store button can vary a little by phone.

How do banners and random items work?

Some banners offer random items in exchange for Stars or tickets. The game shows the odds for each rarity before you pull. Free pulls are available on a timer. Paid random pulls are turned off when the store country or the device region is Belgium. That check stays on the device.

What about Game Center?

On iPhone and iPad the game can sign in to Apple Game Center and report scores and achievements to Apple. That prompt is Apple's. You can dismiss it and keep playing. Android does not use Game Center.

What is the age rating?

The age rating is 9+.

Delete your save or your data

This version of the game does not create an online account. To remove the save on your device, open the game, tap the gear, tap Delete save, then tap Delete forever.

To remove the app's files, uninstall the game. On Android you can clear storage instead: Settings, Apps, K-pop Empire, Storage, Clear storage (some phones say Clear data).

Purchases stay on your Apple ID or Google account. Deleting the game does not cancel a subscription. Cancel steps are in the questions above.

The full deletion page, including how to email a deletion request, is here: Delete your data. That address is the data deletion link for Google Play.
