Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 21, 2026

K-pop Empire: Build Your Agency ("the game") is a mobile game for iPhone, iPad, and Android made by Anelta Igweh ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to information when you play.

Short version: the game keeps your progress on your own device. We do not run accounts. We do not show ads. We do not use analytics or tracking. We do not sell your data.

Information stored on your device

The game saves files in its private storage on your phone or tablet. Other apps cannot read that storage.

  • Your game save: your agency, idols, schedule, and settings. There is one save. The game writes it after each in game day, and when you leave the game.
  • Your Stars, the items you own, banner history, pity counters, and settings such as music volume.
  • A local record of purchases (the product and the transaction id) so a purchase still works when you are offline.

These files stay on the device. This version of the game does not upload them to us or to a cloud save. We cannot see them.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information.

This version has no sign up and no sign in. You play as a guest. Sign in with Apple, Google, and Facebook is not in this version, and the game does not send an account identity to us. A settings screen mentions cloud save as something for a later update. That feature is not active.

The game does not include an advertising SDK, an analytics SDK, or a crash reporting service. In the project, Unity Analytics, Unity Ads, and Unity Cloud Diagnostics are turned off.

The game does not ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone.

The game does use the internet for purchases through Apple or Google, for Apple Game Center on iPhone and iPad, and to open links such as this policy in your browser.

Purchases

Purchases inside the game are handled by the store you downloaded the game from.

  • Android: Google Play Billing. Google collects payment details under the Google Privacy Policy.
  • iPhone and iPad: Apple's App Store. Apple collects payment details under Apple's Privacy Policy.

The game receives a confirmation of what you bought (the product and a transaction id) so it can unlock that item on your device. We do not see your name, email, card number, or billing address. Refunds go through Google Play or Apple.

Subscriptions

VIP Agency and Empire Elite are optional subscriptions sold by Apple or Google. The store charges your Apple ID or Google account. We do not receive your payment details. What a subscription includes, and how to cancel, is on the Support page.

Apple Game Center

On iPhone and iPad, the game can use Apple Game Center. If you sign in to Game Center, the game may send scores and achievement progress to Apple under your Game Center profile. Apple handles that information under Apple's privacy policy. You can dismiss the Game Center prompt and keep playing. The Android version does not use Game Center.

Random items

Some banners sell random items for Stars. The odds are shown in the game before you buy. Paid random pulls are turned off for Belgium. To do that, the game reads your store country and your device region on the device. That check is not sent to us.

Advertising and sale of data

The game has no ads. It does not use an advertising id, and it does not track you across apps or websites. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with advertisers.

Children

The App Store age rating is 9+. The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has sent us information, email us and we will delete it.

Deleting your data

Your game data is on your device, so you control it.

  • Delete the save: open the game, tap the gear icon, tap Delete save, then tap Delete forever. This removes that agency and cannot be undone.
  • Delete the app's files: uninstall the game. On Android you can also open Settings, then Apps, then K-pop Empire, then Storage, then Clear storage (some phones say Clear data).

Purchases stay with your Apple ID or Google account. After you install the game again on a device using that same store account, open the Shop and tap Restore. To cancel a subscription or ask for a refund, use Apple or Google. Steps are on the Support page and on the Delete your data page.

If you believe we hold information about you, email support@kpopempire.net with the subject "Delete my data".

Security

Game files stay in the app's private storage. Purchases travel over the store's encrypted connection. We do not run a player account server for this version of the game.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under the GDPR and the CCPA, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data. Because we do not hold personal data about you, there is usually nothing for us to provide. You can still email us with any request.

Changes

If the game starts to handle data differently, for example by adding cloud save, sign in, analytics, or ads, we will update this page and the effective date before that version is released.
